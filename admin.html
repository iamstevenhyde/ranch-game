End-of-Game Debrief

Row order below comes from RanchEngine.scoreGame(), the live engine's own KPI scorecard: each team's final state weighed against its own archetype's weights, computed fresh from this game's data -- not raw dollars. A team can protect the most equity and still score low, or lose equity and score first, because the scorecard rewards the archetype's own objective, not survival of the balance sheet. Resolved 9/10: the 2026-09-08 class report's own "Rank" column, despite its text calling rank "the game's own scorecard... not the same thing as raw dollars," is in fact a plain ending-cash sort -- verified exactly against the live game for all 8 teams. The Equity panel below shows both orderings side by side (scorecard rank, cash rank in parentheses where they differ) so that gap is visible on the projector, not just asserted: on 9/08, Railli's Rough Riders finished LAST on cash but FIRST on its own archetype's scorecard (rapid_expansion weights herd growth 60%, and their herd went 1,276 to 1,981), while The Mad Cows! finished first on cash but fourth on the scorecard.
1. Bid-to-Anchor: two different questions, two different answers
Anchor is the bull's modeled fair value; 1.0x is a fair-value bid. These are two separate statistics that can point opposite directions -- a team can look disciplined on one and reckless on the other. Median, bids placed is across every bid a team submitted, won or lost. Average, bulls won is only the lots a team actually took home (the class report's own "price / anchor" measure). The gap between them is the winner's curse: a team wins precisely the lots it most overvalued, so its won-lot average runs hotter than its placed-bid median. Lot and bid counts below are read off this game's own tables, not asserted.
2. Cash Bridge: where the money went
Starting cash is after the ranch purchase. Operating net covers calf and bull-sale revenue less feed, labor, payroll, interest and overhead. Components that cannot be reconstructed from the game log fall into "Other / unexplained" rather than being forced to balance. Cows, semen and deals come from the cash ledger the engine now keeps (shipped 2026-09-10); in a game played before that, those three columns read n/a and their dollars stay in Other. This bridge is computed from engine state, not from any report figure.
2b. Equity Bridge: the outcome the class report actually publishes
Equity = cash minus debt plus herd value at the final year's feeder index, the game's own formula and the only outcome measure the class report states (there is no ending-cash figure in the report or the CSV). Where a number here can be checked against the report, it is checked on equity, not cash.
3. Default-Board Counterfactual: what if the team had done nothing?
Held fixed: the team's own ranch and region, the actual world and drought/shock path each year, and the team's actual auction purchases (same bulls, same prices). Operations are replaced with the engine default every year: hold herd, no tech, no AI-semen program, no hires, no debt change, and a default cull. Starting genetics are re-drawn (the actual starting genetics roll was never logged), so this is an approximation, not an audited replay.
